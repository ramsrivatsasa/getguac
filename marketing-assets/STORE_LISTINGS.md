# GetGuac — Store Listings & Beta Readiness

Source copy for the Google Play and Apple App Store submissions, plus a beta
launch checklist. Voice: clean, benefit-first, honest, never preachy. GetGuac is
**free** and is **not** a cashback/points app — it helps people see and save
their **own** money.

---

## Google Play

**Title (30 char max)**
`GetGuac: Receipt & Spending`

**Short description (80 char max)**
`Scan receipts, see where your money goes & never miss a refund. Free.`

**Full description (≤4000 char)**
```
Take control of your money — for free.

GetGuac turns your everyday receipts into a clear picture of where your money
goes, so you can keep more of it. Snap a receipt and Guac-AI reads every line in
seconds — no typing, no spreadsheets.

WHAT GETGUAC DOES
• Scan any receipt — photo, PDF, or forwarded email receipt
• Reads bank statements too, so the whole picture lives in one place
• Auto-categorizes spending and catches duplicate receipts
• GuacScore: one simple 0–100 read on how you’re spending
• Catches hidden fees and the subscriptions you forgot you’re paying for
• Tracks returns and refund deadlines so you never leave money behind
• Steals: finds a cheaper price on the things you rebuy
• Shareable shopping lists — build one on the fly, send it to family in a tap
• GuacWizard: honest, plain-language money guidance (it never lectures)
• Clear reports and dashboards

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No bank login required. Row-level security on every record. We never sell your
data — your guac stays in your bowl.

ACTUALLY FREE
No subscription, no paywall, no card. GetGuac helps you keep more of your own
money, so the app itself costs you nothing.

Whatever you’re saving for — an emergency fund, your kids’ school, retirement,
or just a little breathing room — GetGuac helps every dollar go further.
```

**Category:** Finance
**Tags/keywords:** receipt scanner, expense tracker, spending tracker, budget, subscriptions, refunds, savings

## Beta Readiness Checklist

**Live & verified**
- [x] Web app on Vercel (getguac.app), force-dynamic dashboard (no stale cache)
- [x] Android APKs published to `/download` (per-ABI + latest.json)
- [x] Marketing surface: home tour, /how-it-works, /tour, /features, /faq,
      /pricing, /about, /contact, /security, /privacy, /terms
- [x] SEO: metadata + OpenGraph/Twitter, JSON-LD (Org, WebSite, SoftwareApp,
      FAQ), sitemap.xml, robots.txt, web manifest, OG image

**Before inviting beta testers**
- [ ] Confirm SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY decision (currently empty — seeding uses
      anon key; confirm prod is fine without it)
- [ ] Smoke-test signup → scan receipt → dashboard on a fresh account
- [ ] Confirm support@ / hello@ / privacy@getguac.app inboxes are monitored
- [ ] Play Console: internal testing track + tester list
- [ ] (iOS) TestFlight build — note: current APKs are debug-signed, not
      Play-ready; a release-signed build is still needed for store submission
- [ ] Crash/analytics: confirm PostHog + Sentry keys set in prod
- [ ] Privacy policy + data-deletion flow linked from store listing (required)

**Known gaps to decide on**
- iOS app: no Apple build in the pipeline yet (Flutter is Android-first today)
- Android APKs are debug-signed — Play submission needs a release keystore
